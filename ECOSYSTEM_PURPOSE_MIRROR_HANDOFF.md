## Installed rule

Every governed entity must establish a contribution to at least one ecosystem-sum class:

`agency | continuity | admissibility | execution | observability | evidence | recovery | interoperability | consequence_containment`

The entity must also preserve explicit authority boundaries, lifecycle truth, consequence boundaries, and evidence/reconstruction semantics.

## Overarching purpose

StegVerse is intended to become governance infrastructure for consequential AI, automation, software, institutional, and machine transitions while preserving agency and preventing learning or innovation from requiring unbounded or irreconstructable consequence.

The ecosystem is not intended to become a universal intelligence provider or a centralized authority for globally correct outcomes.

## Authority preservation

This purpose layer grants no execution, credential, admission, routing, transition, custody, publication, or consequence authority.

Existing repository-local and runtime authority contracts remain controlling.

## Maturity sequence

I. Governed action exists.
II. Governed action generalizes.
III. Governed action becomes interoperable.
IV. Governed action becomes independently verifiable.
V. Governed action becomes expected infrastructure.
