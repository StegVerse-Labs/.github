## One-page customer offer
Review one customer-selected AI-assisted workflow to identify decision authority, information custody, evidence gaps, dependency risks and safe failure behavior. Scope: one intake, one current-state workflow diagram, one authority/evidence inventory, a five-risk priority matrix, a 30-day improvement plan and one review meeting. Customer controls source data. No regulated/sensitive information in public demos; use synthetic examples. This is an assessment, not a certified audit, legal opinion, penetration test or production protocol deployment.

## Discovery script
1. Who owns and approves the workflow's decisions?
2. What information categories and providers enter and leave the workflow?
3. What actions may AI initiate, and which require human approval?
4. What native evidence exists for allow, deny, failure and recovery?
5. What dependencies or credentials could interrupt the workflow?
6. How is consent, retention and deletion handled?
7. Who accepts the findings and what remediation budget/timeline applies?
