## Purpose

Formalize and falsify the proposition that architectural difference alone is not a sufficient StegVerse denial basis when a candidate independently satisfies the governing requirement under the same valid evidence standard.

## Collision result

Current GTG semantics already require substantive evaluation of standing, authority, evidence, policy, constraints, and commit-time validity, but no first-class invariant was found that makes incumbent-path conformity itself an inadmissible denial reason. Completed Gate Legitimacy and Independent Review formalisms are adjacent evidence surfaces and do not duplicate this candidate-level predicate.

No competing branch, open PR, or equivalent implementation was observed before claim.

## Required evidence

- deterministic architecture-neutral admissibility predicate;
- same requirement / different valid evidence-path positive cases;
- explicit conformity-only denial falsification;
- valid substantive denials for evidence, authority, standing, safety, policy, and commit-time insufficiency;
- proof that architectural difference neither grants nor removes admissibility;
- preservation of GTG authority boundaries.

## Coordination boundary

Architecture neutrality does not weaken safety, evidence, authority, standing, policy, constraints, or commit-time requirements. It does not require acceptance of all architectures. It only forbids using architectural nonconformity as a substitute for an otherwise missing substantive denial basis.
