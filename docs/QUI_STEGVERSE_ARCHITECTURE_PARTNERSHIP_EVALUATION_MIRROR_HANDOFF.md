3. Present StegVerse boundary: evidence, interpretation, governance authority, state transition, execution, and receipt custody are separable concerns.

5. Compare evidence custody: provenance, retained receipts, replay/reconstruction, and how disputed observations are handled.

7. Identify possible interfaces: cognitiveOS as an intelligence/memory participant behind a governed StegVerse transition boundary; StegVerse as governance/evidence/execution control around QUI-originated intents; or a neutral interoperability test harness.
