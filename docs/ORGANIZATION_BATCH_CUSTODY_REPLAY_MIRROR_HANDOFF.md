## Purpose
Clarify the existing organizational receipt hierarchy: individual state transitions remain replayable at organization level; independently verified bounded organization batches are delivered to Master Records for cross-organization organization records and complex reconstruction. This is a refinement of previous documentation, not a new authority plane or production execution claim.

## Ownership
WorkerCoordinator retains worker claim/fence, expiry and reassignment. Task Registry owns durable task state and successor obligations. Interlock/InTr owns transition governance; TV/TVC owns credentials. The existing organization ledger owns exact locally reconstructable worker/task/sequence receipts and hash-linked batches. Master Records verifies batches as organization records and reconstructs cross-organization histories. The Organization preserves durable global custody; Master Records acquires no transition authority.

## Failure and expiry
A genuinely irrecoverable worker failure must preserve its exact evidence and last closed transition, expire the existing fenced assignment through WorkerCoordinator, update canonical Task Registry with durable failure and successor owner, record the worker-expiry receipt in the existing organization ledger, and trigger immediate batch closure. An individual blocked work item does not automatically expire a worker with other lawful work. When custody itself fails, preserve the incident on existing durable worker/task failure surfaces, with pending custody explicitly marked; do not fabricate a completed state transition or introduce another failure mailbox.

## Batch boundaries
Use one ordered hash-linked organization ledger. Batch closure may be routine (time/size after measurement), task closure, worker expiry, consequential governed transition, recovery of pending custody, or inter-organization handoff. Batch classification is metadata, not separate ledgers. A batch commits exact organization identity, contiguous sequence range, prior batch hash, first/last org receipt hashes, ordered receipt hashes/commitment, required-evidence commitment, closure reason, cross-boundary predecessors and Master Records organization-record acknowledgement status. Validate no omission, duplication, broken predecessor or reorder; retain unacknowledged batches locally and use existing authorized transport with idempotent retry.

## Replay
Local organization replay must reconstruct individual worker, task and sequence receipts and required evidence without requiring Master Records to be online. Master Records must independently verify organization batches and reconstruct relationships spanning organizations. Where a transition contract explicitly requires immediate Master Records acknowledgement, local-only replay or queued batch submission cannot authorize progression.

## 2026-09-29 manifested receipt packet: t(0) establishment and HB(Δ) expiry

Receipt batching before this change had no lower bound and no origin. A packet existed only by derivation — `open_packet_state` counted receipts since the last batch — so an assignment that produced no receipts had no packet at all, and a packet below the manifest COUNT could remain open indefinitely. Both are the same lost-receipt condition: receipts (or their absence) that never reach custody and are not detectable as overdue. The `ROUTINE_THRESHOLD` closure reason survived as a constant with no evaluator after the COUNT policy superseded it, so nothing bounded an open packet.

**The packet is now established, not derived, and records its own establishment.** A manifested receipt packet's first receipt carries `boundary_evidence.receipt_packet_establishment`: the establishment kind, the establishment heartbeat, the declared Δ, and the derived expiry heartbeat. That t(0) accounting is what distinguishes *establishment never occurred* from *a failure occurred after establishment*; without it an absent packet is ambiguous between the two. A packet is therefore never empty — member #1 is its own establishment record — and expiry is computable from the packet alone with no external lookup.

**Release and successor establishment are one transition and one receipt.** That receipt is member #1 of the packet it opens, so the release holds its own identity instead of riding as an attribute of an unrelated work transition. The released batch and its execution-evidence carriage result (COMPLETED/FAILED, `governance_disposition` still null) are retained on that establishment receipt. The successor packet initializes at establishment + 1 rather than at 1. Two birth paths exist and both produce a self-describing member #1: `MANIFEST_ASSIGNMENT_T0` for the four-part WorkerCoordinator transition (task assignment / worker / manifest / manifested receipt packet, one transition with four parts sharing one establishment heartbeat), and `PRIOR_PACKET_RELEASE` for every packet opened by the release of its predecessor. A t(0) establishment that would also release a prior packet fails closed.

**Expiry is a countdown on the existing heartbeat and requires nothing else.** `control/heartbeat-protocol-anchor.json` declares `progression_dependency: OSCILLATOR_ONLY`, `continuous_process_required: false` and `missed_references_continue_to_exist: true`; `heartbeat_runtime/independent_oscillator.py` derives the epoch from a fixed anchor at a 10 ms period. HB is computed, never sampled, so Δ is evaluable by any participant at any time with no scheduler, sampler, timer or live process, and whether or not the organization has appended since. An expired packet closes below COUNT under the new `MANIFEST_RELEASE_DELTA_EXPIRY` reason, distinguishable from a count-satisfied `MANIFEST_RELEASE_CONDITION` closure. Release authority is granted once, by the manifest, at establishment (`release_authorized_at_establishment`), so nothing is adjudicated when the condition fires — which is why HB observing the condition does not give HB any progression authority. The existing organization append owner still performs the release under the existing ledger lock.

**Scope and compatibility.** `receipt_batch.establishment` is optional. A manifest that does not declare it stays count-governed and behaves exactly as before, so manifests written before packets had a t(0) remain valid; a manifest that does declare one requires its packets to carry the t(0) record and fails closed otherwise. No new ledger, runtime, scheduler, dispatcher, WorkerCoordinator, custody store, authority plane, source transport, credential route or device dependency is introduced, and `heartbeat_runtime` was already carried by the existing resident source allowlist. Positive and adversarial cases cover t(0) contents, the expiry boundary at Δ-1 and Δ, the short expiry-closed batch, establishment as member #1 with the work receipt at member #2, retention of a FAILED carriage on the establishment receipt, a manifested packet with no t(0), invalid heartbeat and Δ declarations, and the double-t(0) refusal.

Source/CI validation does not establish an authentic runtime disposition. The first live establishment, expiry and release still require invocation with the existing authorized resident ledger and custody configuration; all applicable runtime evidence remains `UNKNOWN_NOT_AUTHENTICALLY_OBSERVED`.

If the declaration is malformed or mismatched, the ingress refuses through the existing recorded-refusal path with the owner's failure code.
- **Shared heartbeat.** New organization receipts carry `hb_reference` from `org-kernel/kernel.py`, loaded the same way the repository emitter loads it. A supplied `hb_epoch` gives both levels of one ingress the same reference. When it is absent, the reference is derived and marked `derived_from_clock: true`. The schema stays `stegverse.organization-transition-receipt/v1`, and `observed_at` stays as non-ordering provenance. Exact-source retry still returns the original receipt, because neither `hb_reference` nor `observed_at` is compared. Historical v1 receipts without `hb_reference` still verify, replay and pass the three schema consumers.
- **Typed partial commit.** If the organization append raises after the repository receipt is written, the exception does not escape. The ingress returns `FAIL_CLOSED` / `ORGANIZATION_APPEND_NOT_COMMITTED` / `ORGANIZATION_RECEIPT_APPENDED_UNDER_LOCK`. `repository_receipt_committed` is `true` only after the receipt has been read back by its digest from the repository ledger store, and is `UNKNOWN_NOT_AUTHENTICALLY_OBSERVED` otherwise. `organization_receipt_committed` comes from a readback of the organization ledger's HEAD and receipts. `organization_runtime_transition_committed` is `false`. The record also carries the retry entrypoint, the owning goal, the next attempt and evidence references. It never reports both levels as uncommitted.

**Evidence class: SOURCE_IMPLEMENTED, with local test results only** (temporary ledgers, SDK and crossing replaced with fixtures). Nothing here observed a runtime append: no authentic invocation, organization receipt, ledger readback or Master Records record is claimed, and every applicable runtime disposition stays `UNKNOWN_NOT_AUTHENTICALLY_OBSERVED`. This change does not modify `.stegverse/transition-ledger/emit.py`. Repository-level exact retry (`idempotent_on`) arrived on `main` separately through PR #3020, and the merge of `main` into this branch keeps it. `_append_both_levels` passes `idempotent_on` to the repository append and gives the organization receipt the retained repository receipt's epoch. A retry after a partial commit therefore reuses the recorded repository receipt and completes the organization level once. `resident-runtime/organization_egress_boundary.py` also still calls `organization_ledger.append(...)`. ## 2026-10-09 OL-1b: the Organization manifest declares the ledger locus

Approved in StegVerse-Labs/TVC#488 (comment 6084480922, answering 6084332899). This task owns `ledger_store.py` and `organization_store()` (Q-OL6).

- **Store selection.** The Organization ledger locus is declared by the Organization manifest, not chosen by the host. `.stegverse/transition-ledger/org-contract.json` declares `organization_ledger` = `{"store":"git","ref":"refs/heads/organization-ledger/StegVerse-Labs","custody":"PRIVATE","propagate_target":null}` (the ref is in G1-R's `GitLedgerStore` namespace and passes its ref validation). `resident-runtime/aggregate_repo_transition.py::organization_store()` reads that declaration first. `STEGVERSE_ORG_LEDGER_STORE`, `_GIT_REF`, `_GIT_REMOTE` and `_GIT_CUSTODY` are non-authoritative: each one present must equal the declared value, otherwise the append is refused FAIL_CLOSED `ORGANIZATION_LEDGER_LOCUS_MISMATCH` before any store is built, so nothing is appended. With the approved `null`, the store has no remote and `GitLedgerStore.propagate` returns its existing typed non-gating FAIL_CLOSED `ORGANIZATION_LEDGER_REMOTE_NOT_SUPPLIED`, while the local compare-and-swap append completes and the chain continues. A caller that already holds a store or root still passes it directly, as before.
